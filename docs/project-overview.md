# ✈️ Chartered Flight Database Management System (CFDBMS)

## 1. Project Introduction

The Chartered Flight Database Management System (CFDBMS) is an academic database development project based on the case study of Impact Charter Services (ICS).

Impact Charter Services is a private aviation company providing charter flight services to individuals, business executives, tourists and organisations.

The project focuses on designing a centralised database system to support the company's operational and administrative activities.

## 2. Existing Business Problems

Currently, the company manages its daily operations using spreadsheets, paper-based forms and independently maintained administrative records.

This approach creates several operational challenges:

- Duplicate booking records.
- Inaccurate customer information.
- Delayed invoicing.
- Limited visibility of aircraft availability.
- Conflicting pilot schedules.
- Inefficient financial and management reporting.

## 3. Project Aim

The main aim is to design and develop a Chartered Flight Database Management System that improves data management, operational efficiency, accuracy and information security.

## 4. Project Objectives

The proposed system aims to:

1. Manage customer and passenger information.
2. Record and manage charter flight bookings.
3. Maintain aircraft information.
4. Manage pilot and crew records.
5. Support crew assignments.
6. Record financial transactions.
7. Manage aircraft maintenance activities.
8. Maintain data integrity and minimise scheduling conflicts.

## 5. Main System Components

The proposed database will cover the following areas:

- Customer Management
- Passenger Management
- Charter Booking Management
- Aircraft Management
- Pilot Management
- Crew Assignment Management
- Financial Management
- Aircraft Maintenance Management

## 6. Expected Benefits

The proposed system is intended to:

- Reduce duplicate records.
- Improve booking accuracy.
- Centralise operational information.
- Support efficient scheduling.
- Improve financial record management.
- Support better business decision-making.
